# DroneDream community guidelines

Effective date: July 25, 2026

The DroneDream community exists to exchange reproducible PX4, Gazebo, flight
control, optimization, and engineering evidence. Participation should make the
work safer, clearer, and easier for another person to inspect.

## Publish evidence responsibly

- Describe the vehicle, software version, route or scenario, parameters,
  constraints, and expected result when they matter to the discussion.
- Separate measured results from assumptions and clearly label simulated,
  bench, and real-flight evidence.
- Do not present unreviewed community advice as a flight-safety approval.
- Do not encourage bypassing geofencing, aviation law, operator authorization,
  or hardware safety controls.

## Protect people and systems

- Never post passwords, API keys, access tokens, private certificates, precise
  personal locations, confidential logs, or another person's personal data.
- Upload only images and material that you are allowed to share.
- Do not upload malware, tracking payloads, deceptive links, or content intended
  to compromise another system.
- Do not use the community to coordinate illegal, dangerous, harassing,
  hateful, or abusive activity.

## Keep discussion useful

- Do not spam, automate bulk posting, manipulate likes, impersonate another
  person, or repeatedly publish the same content.
- Critique experiments and conclusions rather than attacking contributors.
- Use clear titles and keep replies relevant to the engineering question.
- Disclose important conflicts, sponsored claims, or unverifiable promotional
  material.

## Moderation and reporting

Content may be limited or removed, and accounts may be restricted, when needed
to protect users, maintain service reliability, respond to legal obligations,
or enforce these guidelines. Moderation is not an endorsement or technical
verification of content that remains visible.

Report security issues through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Report community
abuse, privacy concerns, copyright concerns, or deletion requests to
**cz005623@gmail.com** and include the topic URL or enough detail to identify
the content.
